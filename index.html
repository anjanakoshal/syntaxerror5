📖 Offline Monsoon Survival & First-Aid Protocols OFFLINE READY
⚡ Submerged Power Lines & Electrocution Avoidance ▼
CRITICAL RULE: Never step into water if fallen wires or bubbling sparks are visible within 50 feet. Water conducts high-voltage current silently.
  • Step-Potential Hazard: If caught in electrified water, keep your feet together and shuffle/hop away. Never take large steps.
  • Submerged Transformers: Avoid steel electricity poles and basement meter rooms completely.
  • In-house Safety: Turn off the main electrical breaker switch with a dry wooden stick before water reaches wall sockets.
💧 Emergency Safe Drinking Water Purification ▼

Floodwaters are contaminated with sewage and leptospirosis bacteria. Follow these field purification guidelines:

  • Boiling (Gold Standard): Bring cloudy water to a rolling boil for a minimum of 3 full minutes.
  • Chlorine / Halazone Tablets: Add 1 tablet (500mg) per 20 Litres of clear water. Stir and wait 30 minutes before drinking.
  • Emergency Bleach Dosage: In extreme survival: Add 2 drops of unscented household bleach (5% sodium hypochlorite) per 1 Litre of water. Wait 30 minutes.
🐍 Snakebite Emergency Protocol in Floods ▼
Reptiles seek dry ground during floods (tree branches, roofs, furniture). Stay alert when wading in murky water.
  • Immobilize Limb: Keep the bitten limb completely still and positioned below the heart level.
  • DO NOT CUT OR SUCK: Never cut the wound, apply ice, or suck the venom.
  • DO NOT USE TOURNIQUETS: Do not tie tight rubber tourniquets (causes limb tissue necrosis). Apply a firm crepe pressure bandage over the entire limb instead.
  • Rush to Camp: Note snake markings if possible and transport victim calmly to St. Mary High School (Stocked with Anti-Snake Venom).
🛶 DIY Floating Rafts & Buoyancy Devices ▼
  • Plastic Canister Raft: Tie 4 to 6 empty, tightly sealed 20-litre plastic water cans together with rope underneath a wooden door or bamboo frame to support 2 adults.
  • Heavy Duty Tarpaulin Bundle: Place dry grass, inflated air-pillows, or empty sealed bottles inside a thick tarp, folded and lashed like a coracle.
  • Pants Buoyancy Trick: Tie knots in the legs of denim jeans, zip up, and swing through the air over your head to trap air pockets inside the legs for an instant emergency life vest.
